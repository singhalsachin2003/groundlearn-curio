# Wording for the optional parent email (Curio Island)

## Privacy page (groundlearn.com/curio/privacy), add this section
**Parent email (optional).** When a grown-up buys Ad-free, the app asks, behind the grown-up check, whether they would like to share an email address. It is optional and the purchase works without it. We use it only to send the grown-up news about Curio Island and GroundLearn. It is never asked of the child, never shown in the game, never used for ads, and never shared or sold. It is stored securely with our database provider (Supabase). To have it deleted, write to the support address on the Play listing.
